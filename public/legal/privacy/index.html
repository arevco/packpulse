PackPulse Privacy

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how PackPulse processes operational, account, and connected service data, including data received from QuickBooks Online when a customer chooses to connect that integration.

Effective April 2, 2026
Service: PackPulse by REV Copack

Summary

PackPulse uses customer data to operate production visibility workflows, prepare invoice review data, support integrations like QuickBooks Online, and secure and improve the platform. PackPulse does not sell customer data.

1. Information We Process

  • Account and workspace details, including user email addresses and session information.
  • Operational data uploaded or synchronized into PackPulse, such as production, work order, inventory, and item master data.
  • Connected accounting data from QuickBooks Online, such as customer names, item records, term records, company realm IDs, and invoice-related mappings.
  • System logs, usage events, error traces, and technical diagnostics needed to maintain the service.

2. How We Use Information

  • Operate PackPulse features and customer workspaces.
  • Connect to QuickBooks Online and other authorized third-party services.
  • Prepare, preview, synchronize, and track invoice-related data and accounting mappings.
  • Secure the platform, troubleshoot issues, monitor performance, and improve reliability.
  • Communicate about service operations, implementation, support, and account administration.

3. QuickBooks Online Data

When a customer connects QuickBooks Online, PackPulse may access and store data needed to support the approved workflow, including customer catalog records, item catalog records, terms, company identifiers, and invoice export metadata. Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored server-side and are not exposed in client-side code.

4. How Information Is Shared

PackPulse may share information with service providers that support hosting, storage, observability, and connected platform functionality, including infrastructure and API providers required to operate the service. Information may also be shared when required by law, to protect the service, or at the customer's direction.

5. Data Retention

PackPulse retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain customer workspaces, satisfy legitimate business and security needs, and comply with legal or contractual obligations. Connected service metadata and audit records may be retained to prevent duplicate exports and support accounting traceability.

6. Security

PackPulse uses administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, and loss. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Customer Choices and Access

Customers control whether they connect QuickBooks Online and which accounting data is pushed from PackPulse into that system. Customers may also request updates or removal of workspace information through their PackPulse administrator or implementation contact, subject to legal, contractual, and operational limitations.

8. Changes to This Policy

REV Copack may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or PackPulse data handling should be directed through the customer's PackPulse implementation contact or workspace administrator.
